Get timely expert help when submitting your learner data to the ESFA from HT Projects Ltd

Are you a levy-paying employer or training provider with a new ESFA contract and feel overwhelmed with submitting your learner data to the ESFA?

A lot of new contract holders need help understanding the relevant parts of the ILR specification and Provider Guidance, funding and management (FAM) codes and other ILR based systems and terminology.


We can help.

The team at HT Projects have been helping training providers since 1995.

We will assist you with getting the learner details into the ESFA ILR Learner Tool and producing an ILR XML file with no errors that can be uploaded to the ESFA Information Management Services (IDAMS) system.

What’s included?

We provide a service where one of our experienced team will be on hand to train you and your staff with the following:-

ESFA ILR Learner Entry Tool
Funding Information System - FIS
Learning Aims Reference Service (LARS) and its replacement ‘Finding a learning aim’
The Data Service and ILR Guidance
Learner data issues and errors from FIS and/or Information Management Services (IDAMS)

Learners personal information is handled with the utmost of care. No data will be transmitted between us in an insecure manner.
